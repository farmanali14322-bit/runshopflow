01

The phone rings while you're working.

See how this happens
Most callers won't leave a voicemail — they're already scrolling for the next number. If you don't text back inside the first five minutes, the win-rate falls off a cliff. By 4pm, the job's gone.
02

Quote forms die after 3 days.

You meant to follow up Monday. Now it's Thursday and the customer found someone else.

See how this happens
Three days is the median window before a quote goes cold. Most shops touch the lead once — maybe twice — and then it slides off the to-do list. Meanwhile the customer is comparing you to two other shops who replied in an hour.
03

Nobody asks for the review.

Your competitor with 247 reviews wins every Google search. You've done 2,000 jobs and have 31 reviews.

See how this happens
The customers who'd leave the best reviews almost never do — they're happy, they pay, they drive off. The reviews you do get skew toward the unhappy minority. Without a systematic ask in the 24 hours after a job, you stay invisible to the next searcher.
